Last updated: 2025

ColorPulse (“the App”) is a mobile game developed by an individual developer. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user data. By using the App, you agree to the terms outlined here.

The App does not collect, store, or process any personal information such as:

  • name or surname,
  • email address,
  • phone number,
  • location data,
  • photos, files, or contacts.

We also do not create user accounts or store any identifiers on our servers (we do not have a server).

2. Non-Personal Data

The game may collect non-personal technical information for analytics or advertising:

  • device model,
  • operating system version,
  • language settings,
  • approximate region (country),

This information is anonymous and cannot identify you personally.

3. Advertising

The App may display ads provided by third-party advertising networks. These networks may use anonymous identifiers (such as advertising ID) to:

  • limit ad frequency,
  • provide non-personalized advertising,
  • perform aggregated analytics.

Potential ad providers include (depending on what developer enables):

  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads / LevelPlay (ironSource)

The App itself does not read or store Advertising ID — it is handled only by the ad network SDK.

4. Children’s Privacy

The App does not target children under 13 and does not collect personal data from children.

5. Data Security

We do not store any personal data on servers. Anonymous technical data collected by ad networks is protected according to their policies.

6. Third-Party Links

The App does not contain external links except for this Privacy Policy page.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available on this page.
